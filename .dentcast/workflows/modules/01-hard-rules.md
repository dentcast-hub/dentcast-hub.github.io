# DentCast Publishing Router

## Hard rules

1. **Brain is the source of truth.** Every content type's structure, required fields, schema, directory, URL pattern and media requirements are inferable from `dentcast-brain.json`. Read it **once per run** (Phase A); later steps use that loaded copy plus this run's own edits.
2. **Categories are isolated.** Never use one category's entry as a template for another or borrow another type's schema shape.
3. **New entries go to the absolute END of the flat `dentcast-brain.json` array.** The brain is one flat array (no per-type sections) and the latest-content widget reads its last 30 entries across all types, so the new entry becomes the last element of the whole file, whatever its type. This is the only home of the tail rule.
4. **Auto-discover first, ask second.** Ask the founder only for data you cannot infer (Hard Rule 13).
5. **Don't invent fields.** A new brain entry has exactly the keys of the previous same-category entry — no more, no less. Scores, pathways and flashcards live in their own files, never in the brain.
6. **Pillar & subtopic are decided once, at step 2.4, and never re-decided.** Step 2.4 picks `pillar.primary` from the **live** pillar set (`list(PILLARS.keys())`) and, for a structured pillar, `pillar.subtopic` from that pillar's own subtopics, by semantic match (Hard Rule 14). An ambiguous case takes the agent's closest semantic match, recorded with its reason (standing acceptance); the founder may change it later. Steps 2.5 and 5 consume the decision; step 5 records it and step 5.5 / Phase F only verify it. Nothing downstream re-classifies, re-guesses or overwrites it.
7. **Structured-pillar list is live, never hardcoded.** Only some pillars are *structured*: `tools/build_pillar.py` builds `/pillar/<slug>/index.html` (the flat, date-ordered list everyone sees) and `/pillar/<slug>/structure.json` (the subtopic foldering `/pillar/premium-index.js` layers on for premium subscribers). A subtopic is a premium-side arrangement, not a page section — it must still be right (the میز کار tree and the premium view read it). Read the structured set from `PILLARS` in `tools/build_pillar.py` on every run; non-structured pillars get no subtopic. Never bake pillar slugs, subtopic slugs or Persian titles into this workflow. A new subtopic is a founder decision (asked when it arises); a new pillar is never created at step 2.4.
8. **`main` is authoritative for taxonomy and builders.** Read `PILLARS` and run any tool from a working state that reflects `main` (run from `main`, or merge `main` first). Never trust a feature branch's `tools/build_pillar.py`.
9. **Hard cap of 5 links per «کاوش بیشتر» section.** One budget of **at most 5 links**, counting every link of every kind in that section: the nav capsules (دانشنامه → sitewide `/glossary/`; فهرست موضوعی → the entry's **own** pillar `/pillar/<pillar.primary>/`), glossary back-links and semantic brain-entry links. Before adding, count what is there; at 5 add nothing; below 5 add at most `5 − count`, highest-priority candidates first. Step 2.5's capsules are placed first; 4.7 (on glossary pages), 4.9 and LiteCast's 0.6 fill only what remains. **Only add — never remove, reorder or restyle existing links to make room.** Every step touching such a section defers to this rule.
10. **LiteCast is an isolated track.** LiteCast is general-audience content with its **own** source of truth (`litecast/lite-glossary.json`, not the brain) and its own internal links. It is never cross-linked with the specialist ecosystem in either direction, but links **among its own items**: its «کاوش بیشتر» holds only other LiteCast entries (no دانشنامه / فهرست موضوعی capsules, no glossary terms, no brain entries). When the locked category is LiteCast, Phase C step 0 overrides the general steps. **Single-domain identity:** every LiteCast page lives on `https://dentcast.ir` — full `.ir` profile (canonical, `og:url`, JSON-LD `/litecast/` values), **no hreflang**, root-relative internal links — while every other category stays on `https://dentcast.org`. The clone carries this (Phase C step 2 identity invariant) and `.github/scripts/inject_hreflang.py` skips `.ir`-canonical pages.

11. **Enrichment steps (4.7, 4.8, 4.9) are MANDATORY and template-independent — on every publish, for every type, core episodes included.** This is the only home of the clone rule:
    - **The step-2 clone and any same-category precedent govern STRUCTURE ONLY — never enrichment.** "The previous/cloned page had no inline links / only the pillar capsule, so I matched it" is a forbidden justification; a gap on earlier pages is something to fix, never a pattern to copy forward (this applies to every backlog: Pulse lines, toggles, credits). A link that merely survived the clone does not count as executing 4.8/4.9.
    - **Each step runs a fresh semantic analysis and ends explicitly resolved.** Strong candidates are applied, borderline ones are skipped and recorded (Hard Rule 14). The only valid no-op is a documented empty result ("4.8: analyzed body, 0 qualifying candidates" / "4.9: section at cap"); silence is never a state.
    - **Core episodes** have no `dc-related-section`: step 4.9 targets their «محتوای مرتبط» block (`ep-related-link`, sibling parts of a multi-part series first, under Hard Rule 9) and step 4.8 targets the «درباره این اپیزود» caption. The naming difference is not a reason to skip.

12. **Every `.org` page ships bilingual: the English mirror + fa↔en toggle is MANDATORY.** The publish is incomplete until the page has a real English counterpart at `/{type}/en/{same-filename}.html` and a working toggle (`.lang-btn`) on both sides; a toggle whose target is not on disk is a forbidden phantom pair. The mirror is authored before C.6 from the locked text (module 12 Phase D, `.dentcast/workflows/en-version.md`) and ships `noindex, follow`, without hreflang and outside the sitemap (an unreviewed AI translation, a reader feature, not a search surface). **Two exceptions, and no others:** LiteCast (`.ir`-only, no hreflang, Hard Rule 10), and a **چالش** (`.dentcast/challenge-handoff.md` RULE 16): its mirror would be a different `content_id` with no row in the `challenges` table, so the toggle would lead to a page missing the only thing on it. For either, Phase D is a documented skip. This is the only home of that reason.

13. **Never guess — one binding rule for the whole workflow.** A fact you do not have from (a) the founder, (b) a file in the repo, (c) the live taxonomy, or (d) a real source you fetched or probed (the audio file, the PDF, a PubMed/Crossref lookup, a Drive listing) is unknown: never estimate, infer, round or fabricate a stand-in. This gates only the dependent action, never the whole publication.
    - **Where it is asked.** Intake data the founder supplies (type when the request does not make it obvious, text, media, paper file, the چالش content, the DES basket-3 choice) is requested at intake only when missing. Every other open fact (DOI, first author, which Drive file, catalog counters, a duration) goes into **message M1 at C.4.13** (`publish-checkpoints.md`); the dependent action waits, independent work continues. Once answered, record it and never ask again.
    - **Numeric/factual metadata — duration, file size, dates, counts, IDs, DOIs, first authors — is never estimated.** If the audio host is unreachable and the duration cannot be probed, it is an open fact; no placeholder goes into the brain, `dentcast.json`, the page's visible time or JSON-LD `duration`, not even temporarily.
    - **Schema-shape choices that affect site behaviour are never resolved by picking the closer precedent.** When two precedents could govern a new entry's field set, that is an open fact: a wrong pick can silently break automation that keys off fields (the homepage hero keys off `type` and `audio_url`).
    - **Explicit founder instructions are scoped approval.** A direct request or choice is approval for exactly that scope: record it, do not ask again, do not extend it (a request to add a named hashtag concept in Persian and English authorizes resolving those variants through the library, not unrelated tags).
    - **A report is no substitute for asking.** Disclosing an assumed fact afterwards ships unconfirmed data; an open fact is asked before writing. (Judgment calls are a different case: Hard Rule 14.)

14. **Standing acceptance for judgment calls — auto-apply strong, conservative-skip borderline, record both.** Agent proposals (the title or caption the agent composed, the lead paragraph, Q3.5 rewrites, the Pulse line, hashtags and mints, keywords, pillar/subtopic, link candidates at 0.5/0.6/4.7/4.8/4.9, flashcards and FAQ, catalog tags and Drive subfolder, pathway placement, bundle gates, the English mirror) are **never sent to the founder for confirmation** (`publish-checkpoints.md`, «Standing acceptance»). The agent builds each per its module and records it with evidence in the handoff; report G lists them and the founder may change any afterwards (that change follows the invalidation rules). It never relaxes Hard Rule 13: derivability, not confidence, decides whether a fact may be written.
    - **Every judgment is semantic** — conceptual relevance in context, **never** keyword/string matching — for pillars, subtopics, links, Drive subfolders, tags and pathways alike.
    - **Strong** (the matched concept is unmistakably the one discussed; exactly one pillar is a clear fit; the next number with no gap; a Pulse line in the type's established no-spoiler pattern) → apply it.
    - **Borderline** (a tangential link, a third pathway, an uncertain bundle gate, an ambiguous FAQ pair) → the conservative default: not applied, recorded with its reason. Ambiguity that needs *some* value (pillar/subtopic, Drive subfolder among existing ones) → the closest semantic match, recorded with its reason.
    - **Reporting is mandatory either way:** report G says what was applied, what was skipped and why.

15. **Hashtags are a standardized vocabulary — `dentcast-hashtag-reference.json` is their single source of truth.** Every `#tag` in a brain entry (and every page chip) is a **canonical concept** of that library (mirrored into `plus/content-index.json` by `tools/build_plus_index.mjs`). Resolve first (`python3 tools/hashtag_ref.py --simulate "<phrase>"` finds a concept under any spelling), reuse the canonical `#tag` verbatim if it exists, and mint only if genuinely absent, through a `.dentcast/hashtag-batches/*.json` batch + `hashtag_ref.py --apply`. Four invariants: **an alias is never used as a tag**; **a canonical `#tag` is never filed as another concept's alias**; **no duplicate concept when one already covers it**; **every brain tag has a library entry** (`--apply` enforces it, `--check` must stay green). The concepts themselves are agent proposals under Hard Rule 14; a founder-named concept is scoped approval (Hard Rule 13). The selection gates (dentist questions, composition, resolving sentence, restricted minting, paper bridge) live only in **Phase C step 5.0**; library background: `.dentcast/hashtag-campaign-handoff.md`.

16. **A string the founder supplied is COPIED, never re-typed — codepoint for codepoint.** A title, caption, Pulse sentence, term or any literal text the founder gave ships as that exact character sequence; it is final data, not a proposal. The only edits to founder text are the recorded, style-only Q3.5 rewrites and a Part 3b fix the founder accepted (and never the founder's title or caption). The failures this prevents are invisible on screen:
    - **Persian typography.** A ZWNJ (`U+200C`, نیم‌فاصله) is not a space. «اندو‌شده» ≠ «اندو شده». Likewise NBSP vs space, Arabic ی/ک (`U+064A`/`U+0643`) vs Persian ی/ک (`U+06CC`/`U+06A9`), and any bidi mark.
    - **"Helpful" widening or tightening.** Adding a clause («… از اورهنگ **و روکشِ مجاور** بیاید»), dropping one, or swapping a synonym is a rewrite, not a copy — even when more accurate.
    - **Round-tripping through your own summary.** Copy the string from the founder's message, never reconstruct it.
    - **Mechanically enforced.** The exact title and caption are frozen in the C.6 approvals; Phase F compares every surface with them and names the index and any invisible character:
      ```bash
      python3 tools/verify_publish.py <content_id> \
        --expect-title "<the title exactly as the founder wrote it>" \
        --expect-caption "<the caption exactly as the founder wrote it>"
      ```

17. **A text correction is a SWEEP, never a targeted edit — one string lives on many surfaces.** When a title, caption or description changes after its first write, every surface carrying it changes **in the same commit**.
    - **A title lives in at least eight places:** the brain entry, `<title>`, `<h1>`, `og:title`, `twitter:title`, JSON-LD `headline`, the JSON-LD `BreadcrumbList` item `name`, the landing-page `<li>` label and the homepage rail card. Builder-generated ones are regenerated by the runner's C.8 — never hand-edit them.
    - **A caption lives in at least four:** the brain `caption`, `<meta name="description">`, and (in their own wording) `og:description` + `twitter:description` on **both** the fa page **and** its en mirror.
    - **Never trust recall for the list.** Phase F re-derives every surface from the brain and fails the ones you missed. Before Push 2, sweep and let the runner rebuild; after Push 2 a correction is an invalidation and a new Push 2 (`publish-after-push2.md`), never local builders.

**Evidence of edits.** The handoff writer fingerprints every target path, and `git diff` of the scoped paths is the evidence that an edit changed only what its step allows. No step takes manual `sha256sum` before/after hashes or reports them; a file that must not change (a cloned template, `metanotes/en/meta-1.html`) is checked with `git diff --quiet -- <path>`.
