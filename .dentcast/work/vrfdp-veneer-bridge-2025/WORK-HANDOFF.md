## Source facts to preserve
- DOI https://doi.org/10.32592/3dj.14.1.8 ; Alikhasi et al. 2025; case report with literature review.
- 3 patients; 4 bridges (case 1 had both upper and lower left first premolars missing). «Up to 5 years» follow-up, not a denominator-based 5-year survival estimate and not necessarily five years for each bridge.
- Case 1: 22M; intact adjacent teeth; deep overbite and steep anterior guidance; prior orthodontics left neighboring roots tipped; patient declined repeat orthodontics; monolithic lithium disilicate bridges; maxillary premolar abutment with onlay configuration.
- Case 2: 45F; lower right lateral lost after periodontal problem; 3mm anterior open bite, spacing; extracted tooth as ovate temporary pontic for 2 months; VRFDP and left-incisor veneers; only minor incisal modification in this case.
- Case 3: 21F; upper right lateral missing, peg-shaped upper left lateral, midline diastema, worn centrals; already planned labial veneers, so VRFDP avoids additional palatal preparation.
- Veneer abutments: butt-joint, about 2mm incisal PREPARATION and 0.5–1mm labial PREPARATION in the general description, with case-2 exception; extend adjacent proximal preparations to proximo-lingual line angle.
- Authors cite lithium disilicate manufacturer connector cross section 16mm², height-to-width >1; not universal or prospectively validated instructions.
- No mechanical/biological complications reported in this tiny case series. Older 7 reports include some fractures and other complications; avoid claiming proof of superior material.
