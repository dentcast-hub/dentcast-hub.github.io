# Title
An upper denture that comes loose while eating

# Caption
A patient with a maxillary complete denture and crowned mandibular anterior teeth complains that the denture dislodges while eating. On examination, a premature contact and contact confined to the anterior region were found.

# Body
[[تصویر:01]]

The patient had a complete denture in the maxilla, and in the mandible the teeth up to tooth 4 had been crowned. Their main complaint was that the upper denture is not stable while eating and comes out of place, even when they use denture adhesive.

On examination I saw that when the jaw is placed in the correct relationship, there is a premature contact, and the teeth contact only in the anterior region. In addition, anteriorly, the crowns of the natural lower teeth sit opposite the upper denture. All of these work against the stability of the upper denture.

I explained to the patient that if I also just make a new denture for the upper jaw, the same problem will most likely recur. And if we place implants in the upper jaw and make an overdenture, the denture will no longer move, but instead we will probably face chipping of the prosthetic teeth. So the solution is not to repeat the same previous treatment; both jaws need to be treated at the same time to achieve better support.

The patient had diabetes and also a history of major heart surgery, so implants were removed from the treatment plan. The final treatment plan became removable prostheses for both jaws, together with occlusal correction and correction of the form of the mandibular anterior crowns.
