## Title
Crown lengthening surgery next to an implant

## Caption
Tooth 4 needed crown lengthening surgery to gain an adequate ferrule, but an implant stood next to it. When the tooth itself allows CL, what may still make it impossible?

## Body
A patient came in for a consultation about tooth 4. To have an adequate ferrule, this tooth needed crown lengthening surgery (CL). An implant was located next to this tooth.

[[تصویر:01]]

The root length of the tooth was adequate, and as far as the tooth itself was concerned, CL was feasible. The main limitation was the tooth's proximity to the implant.

In CL, a few millimetres of bone must be removed to create a ferrule, and this removal is also done on the proximal surface. In this patient, the proximal bone between the tooth and the implant provides the implant's bone support on that side. Removing this bone may expose the implant threads and cause the loss of its bone support.

For this reason, I explained to the patient that CL is probably not feasible for this tooth. This was also discussed with a periodontist, who confirmed the same view.

> [[+]] **Clinical tip**
>
> When assessing whether CL is feasible, besides the root length and the condition of the tooth itself, the effect of bone removal on the support of adjacent teeth or implants must also be evaluated.
